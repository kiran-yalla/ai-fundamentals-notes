# 04 - Responsible AI and Ethics Basics

Introductory notes on responsible AI principles — important fundamentals to understand alongside any AI tooling.

## Why Responsible AI Matters

As AI tools become part of everyday engineering and business workflows, it's important to understand their limitations and potential risks, not just their capabilities.

## Core Principles (Basics)

- **Fairness** — AI systems should be evaluated for bias across different groups of people.
- **Transparency** — understanding, where possible, how and why a model produced a given output.
- **Privacy** — being mindful about what data (especially personal or sensitive data) is shared with AI tools and services.
- **Accountability** — a human should remain responsible for decisions made with AI assistance, especially in high-stakes scenarios.
- **Reliability** — testing and validating AI-assisted outputs rather than assuming they are always correct.

## Practical Takeaways for Everyday Use

1. Don't paste confidential or sensitive data into public AI tools unless it's explicitly approved by policy.
2. Always verify important facts, calculations, or code generated by an AI tool before relying on it.
3. Be aware that AI-generated content can carry the biases of its training data.
4. Use AI as an assistant to speed up work, not as an unquestioned source of truth.
